// Cong thuc Raindrop Stratification
X_i^h(t+1) = X_i^h(t)
  + alpha_h(t)·(X_best(t) − X_i^h(t))
  + beta_h(t)·R_i^h(t)

// alpha: luc keo ve best | beta: nhieu ngau nhien

// Levy Flight (Mantegna algorithm)
L(beta) = u / |v|^(1/beta)

u ~ N(0, sigma²), v ~ N(0,1)
sigma = [Gamma(1+beta)·sin(pi·beta/2) /
(Gamma((1+beta)/2)·beta·2^((beta-1)/2))]^(1/beta)

// Surge moi surge_every=15 vong
X_i += surge_str · L(beta=1.5) · (X_max−X_min)

// Dac diem phan phoi Levy
7.1% buoc co |L| > 2.7sigma (duoi nang)
Tai sao can Levy?
Landscape SVM co nhieu local optima. Raindrop/Evaporation co the bi ket.

Levy flight (beta=1.5): phan phoi duoi nang — hau het buoc nho, nhung thinh thoang buoc rat lon de thoat.

Khac Gaussian: Gaussian khong co buoc "dot bien" du lon.
